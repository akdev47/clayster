Design Elements

Text is anything you type yourself.

SVG is a vector drawing, such as a logo or icon. It stays sharp at any size, so it is the best choice for logos.

PNG is a picture made of pixels. Auto works best for simple backgrounds; use Pick background to mark trusted background areas when a gradient or vignette needs help.

A stamp has one colour, so only the shape of an image is used. Its colours are ignored.
